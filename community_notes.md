# Milestone 1 — Community notes

**Community:** r/cscareerquestions (https://www.reddit.com/r/cscareerquestions/)
**Why this one:** I wanted a community where people talk about companies and
what it's actually like to work there. r/cscareerquestions is public, very
active, and its posts range from detailed, number-backed comparisons to pure
venting, so there's real variation in how much evidence a post gives for its
own claims.

**What I read:** Read about 50 posts, sorted by Best (the page's active sort —
not Top → This month), on 26 Oct 2026. Did not read comments, only the main
post text.

## Candidate distinctions

<!-- Three or four things you could point at in the text — not "good vs bad".

1. **Some posts carry a number; most carry a feeling.** The "Which job would
   you take?" post gives exact salary bands, while "AI has made me feel lost"
   has zero quantified data points.

2. **Direct bounded questions vs. open narrative with no real ask.** The
   three-option job post asks for a decision; the corporate-bullshit rant has
   no actual decision embedded in it.

3. **Lopsided vote splits vs. near-even ones.** "Company going full agentic"
   sits at 421 up / 491 down, more contested than its comment count alone would
   suggest.

4. **Edited posts vs. static ones.** The career-crisis post has a visible
   "Edit:" responding to comments; most others don't.
