# baseline.py reads this file.

evidence_backed: the main claim is supported by a specific, checkable fact (a number, dollar figure, named tool, count or named source) and the post does not ask what the poster should personally do
hot_take: a confident, sweeping claim about the industry, AI, a company or a group of people with no specific checkable fact in support, or whose fact could be deleted without weakening the claim
advice_request: the poster describes their own situation and asks an answerable question about a specific decision or task, even if the post contains specific facts
vent: the poster expresses their emotional state about their own situation, with no answerable question and no checkable claim argued; generic closers like "any advice?" don't count as a question
